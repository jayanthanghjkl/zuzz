What ZUZZ remembers

Two tiers, and the difference is the point. Anything in memory only can still be lost — it has not been written anywhere. Only what reaches the disk survives a restart, and only memories scoring at or above 0.50 are eligible to go there.

Importance decides what gets promoted. Facts and preferences are worth keeping; most conversation is not.
Recall

Ranked by IDF-weighted keyword overlap — no model, no embeddings. A word that appears in many memories counts for less than a word that appears in one, which is why a distinctive term finds the right memory where a common word would not.

Everything held

Newest first, with each memory's score against the threshold. The tick on each bar is the threshold; a bar that stops short of the tick is why that memory is still in RAM.

Consolidation

Promotion is the only thing that writes to disk, and it happens only when you ask. ZUZZ does not consolidate on a timer in the background — a background promotion would make the boundary between "in memory" and "permanent" a thing that happens while you are not looking.

Forget

Erasing is irreversible and there is no "forget everything" — you give a cutoff date and only memories older than it go. Preview first: the real button stays disabled until a preview has matched something, so the count you see is the count that gets erased.

Every forget is recorded in the audit trail with the cutoff and the count — never with the content of what was removed.

Mood

Read from the wording, not from anything else. Four moods, each with an instruction the reply has to follow. This is a keyword match over a small vocabulary — it will get "fine, thanks" wrong, and that is the trade for not sending your messages anywhere.
